Architecture & Precision

Integer Quantization

Floating-point math is replaced entirely with fast, deterministic INT8 quantized arithmetic. Parameters are mapped to 8-bit integers (-128 to +127), reducing the footprint to 65 bytes and fitting inside a tiny account segment.

L1 Weights (6 × 8) 48 Bytes
L2 & Biases 17 Bytes

EMA Feature Pre-smoothing

Raw price feeds contain high-frequency micro-noise. Implementing an exponential moving average (EMA) filter boosts on-chain accuracy significantly.
